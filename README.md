# Korrin

Multi-disciplinary creative with a technical lean. I have worked in and around
several creative fields that sit on the line between the technical and the
artistic. After more than a decade as an Apple user, discovering Omarchy moved
me to Linux overnight. Omarchy opens the Linux community to all nerds, not just
the super nerds, and I am already hooked and in deep.

Right now I am building a PC game, and alongside it making assets and
contributing to the Omarchy community.
